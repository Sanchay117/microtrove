The Wave 1 review asked for users beyond our own wallets. These posts are ready to paste.

## Mezo Discord (builders or general channel)

> **Try a $50 bitcoin-backed loan on Mezo Testnet**
>
> Microtrove builds Mezo's RFC-2 microloans: borrow 25 to 200 MUSD against a little BTC,
> funded by an open lender vault, and promote it into a full MUSD trove when you outgrow it.
>
> Looking for a few people to try it this week:
> 1. Open https://microtrove.vercel.app and connect any EVM wallet (it adds Mezo Testnet for you).
> 2. Get testnet BTC from https://faucet.test.mezo.org
> 3. Borrow 25 to 100 MUSD. The guide on the Borrow page walks you through it.
> 4. Repay or close with one signature, or lend MUSD to the vault on the Lend page.
>
> If anything is confusing or breaks, reply here or open an issue:
> https://github.com/Sanchay117/microtrove/issues
